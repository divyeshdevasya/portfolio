Applied ML / Master's thesis

Location-Sequence Research

Research into symbolic movement representations and the relationship between predictive utility and privacy.

Sequence modeling / Data representation / Evaluation

The problem

Location-related modeling has to balance useful behavioral information against the sensitivity and detail of movement data.

Project scope

The thesis, Deep Spatio-Temporal Learning with Anonymity Guarantees, was submitted at TU Darmstadt in June 2025. It explores symbolic spatial representations and compares Markov, LSTM and Transformer approaches alongside preprocessing and privacy-related transformations.

Engineering decisions

  • Study the representation and preprocessing around the model, not only the model architecture.
  • Include a simpler baseline when discussing whether added model complexity is justified.
  • Examine class balance and evaluation assumptions before interpreting aggregate accuracy.

Outcome

A submitted research thesis and a preserved experimental codebase. Selected preprocessing stages have subsequently been exercised using invented data.

Limits and context

The archived training evaluation has unresolved methodological issues. Results are not presented here as independently reproduced next-step forecasting, certified anonymity or production delivery performance.
